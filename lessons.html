Siamese Cat — lesson in progress

An acrylic Siamese cat on a blue ground, shown in nine stages. This one is still on the easel.

  1. The outline — the cat drawn in over the blue ground.
  2. First colour — cream for the body, brown for the points.
  3. The mask — the dark Siamese face mask goes in.
  4. Light and shadow — shaping the body with light and dark.
  5. The eyes — the blue blocked in.
  6. Eye detail — a close look at the eyes.
  7. Face set — the face comes together.
  8. Nose and chest — a pink nose and a lighter chest.
  9. Latest stage — not finished yet.
