Client story · 3D printing & design

Karaage Chicken Bowl — for Ruby at Bro Chicken and Sushi

Ruby / Bro Chicken and Sushi · Hansford Road · Gold Coast

Ruby at Bro Chicken and Sushi on Hansford Road asked for something that doesn’t melt under the lights: a display bowl of their karaage chicken that looks ready to eat all day.

I designed the piece in 3D — chicken, rice, greens, tomato, mayo drizzle — then printed it in parts so the textures could stay crisp. After print, I hand-painted every layer: golden crust on the chicken, juicy tomato cut faces, leaf greens, and that zigzag sauce. A matching KARAAGE CHICKEN label sits in front so customers know the dish at a glance.

It’s fake food made to sell the real thing — design, print, paint, and finish under Gladiuslumen / Anything Art, built for a local Gold Coast kitchen.
