Refunds and exchanges

Shop G003, Runaway Bay Centre, 10–12 Lae Drive, Runaway Bay QLD 4216. Phone 0467 344 801.

Change of mind

Bring it back within 7 days with your receipt. It must be unused and in the same condition you bought it. We give store credit. We do not give a cash refund for a change of mind.

Faulty, or not as described

We will fix it, replace it, or refund what you paid. That is the Australian Consumer Law.

Custom work

A name on a cap, a shirt, or any other item, a custom airbrush design, and anything made to order cannot be returned for a change of mind. If the custom work is faulty, we still fix it, replace it, or refund it.

Classes

Ring 0467 344 801 to move your class and keep what you paid. If you do not come and you do not ring, the payment is kept.
